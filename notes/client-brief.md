# Client brief — Delta Tau Delta, Chi Chapter (Kenyon College)

I'm the chapter president, so this is my own chapter.

## Who runs the site
The president would be the admin. The secretary, the social media chairs, and the alumni relations chair would be editors. They are all college students, so reasonably comfortable with computers, but most are not highly technical. These roles all change hands usually once a year.

## Who reads it, and what they want
Prospective members, alumni, parents, and Kenyon staff. They want to know about the members of the chapter, just updates, our community service, history, etc.

## What's broken now
We have a very outdated website. It's run by one alum who never updates it. Alumni can't find updates, info is very spread out, and active members can't make any changes.

## What would make it "better"
Active members can easily make changes when need be, and alumni can find what the chapter is currently up to.

## What must NOT happen
- A graduated officer can still log in and post.
- An editor deletes or changes the chapter history page, or another officer's post.
- A half-written draft goes public (like a rush announcement with the wrong date).
- Members' personal info ends up public.
