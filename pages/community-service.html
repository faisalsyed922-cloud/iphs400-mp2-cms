Community Service

Chi Chapter has a long history of community service, and we’re happy to report that the tradition remains strong in 2026. Brothers helped the Red Cross harvest over 60 units of blood.

Working in Gund Commons, brothers staff the event with pledges. Enthusiasm in the fraternity is high, with brothers following through on their commitment to giving back to their community.

“Overall, the drives are running smoothly, and we get good attendance,” said Community Service Chair Christophe LeBlanc. “For as long as I’ve been in this role, the brothers are delivering.”
